The Word is a quiet, private Scripture reader. Everything runs on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking.

<b>Read</b>
<ul>
<li>Eight public-domain translations bundled for offline use: King James Version, American Standard Version, World English Bible, Reina-Valera 1909 and 1602, Louis Segond, Chinese Union Version and the 1934 Vietnamese Bible</li>
<li>Interface in English, Spanish, French, Chinese and Vietnamese</li>
<li>Words of Christ in red</li>
<li>Cross-references on every verse</li>
<li>Light and dark themes, adjustable text size, and reading fonts including Atkinson Hyperlegible and OpenDyslexic</li>
</ul>

<b>Find</b>
<ul>
<li>Fast full-text search by all words or exact phrase, filtered by testament or book</li>
<li>Bookmarks you can come back to</li>
</ul>

<b>Listen</b>
<ul>
<li>Read-aloud with your device's own text-to-speech voices, with speed control</li>
</ul>

<b>Share</b>
<ul>
<li>Turn a verse into an image with your choice of background, font and translation</li>
</ul>
